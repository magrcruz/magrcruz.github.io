Undergraduate thesis at Universidad Catolica San Pablo, Arequipa, Peru. Presented as a poster at **Khipu Latin American AI Conference, 2025**.

Current diffusion-based image editing methods are slow and struggle to preserve the original image structure. This work combines two state-of-the-art approaches: **Wurstchen** (compact latent representations for text-guided generation) and **PnP-Diffusion** (feature injection during the denoising process).

The combination allows the model to represent the target text while maintaining the original structure achieving a **14x reduction in editing time** with no significant loss in image quality.
